Frequently asked questions

What is a website pentest?

It is a controlled, authorised attempt to break into your website to find security weaknesses before real attackers do. You receive a report listing what was found and how to fix it.

Do I need to give permission for security testing?

Yes. We only test systems you own or are authorised to test, and we agree the scope in writing first.

What does Windows bypass mean?

It means regaining access to a Windows computer you own when you are locked out. We confirm ownership before starting, and we work to keep your files intact.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the work involved. Send us the details and we’ll give you a clear quote before anything starts.

Can you support me after my website is live?

Yes. We offer maintenance, updates and user support so your site and systems stay secure and working.
